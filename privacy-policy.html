Privacy Policy

Simple Traffic Lights for iOS  ·  Last updated: October 3, 2026

Overview

Simple Traffic Lights is a traffic light simulator for iPhone and iPad. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data We Do Not Collect

Simple Traffic Lights does not collect or share any personal information, and never sends data to us or to any server. Specifically:

  • We do not require or offer any account, sign-in, or profile.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any personal identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs of any kind.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your preferences (mode, colors, timer durations, layout) are kept only in the app's memory on your device while you use it. Nothing is written to a server, and nothing is synced anywhere.

Remote Control and Local Network

The optional remote control feature lets one nearby iPhone or iPad change the traffic light on another. It uses Apple's Multipeer Connectivity framework, which connects the two devices directly over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Because of this, iOS asks for permission to access your local network the first time you use it. If you decline, the rest of the app works normally.

  • The connection is device to device. No internet connection, server, or account is involved.
  • A device only accepts a connection from someone who enters the 4-digit code it shows.
  • The connection is encrypted.
  • The only data exchanged is the current color, which colors are enabled, whether timer mode is on, and commands to change the color. iOS also shares the device's generic name (for example, "iPhone") so the devices can find each other.
  • None of this data is stored, and it is never sent to us or to any third party.
  • Pairing ends when you turn remote control off or close the app.

In-App Purchases

Simple Traffic Lights offers optional, entirely voluntary tips through Apple's In-App Purchase system (StoreKit). These purchases are processed directly by Apple — we never see, receive, or store your payment information. Apple's handling of payment data is governed by Apple's own Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks, aside from Apple's native StoreKit framework used for the optional tips and Apple's Multipeer Connectivity framework used for remote control, both described above.

Children's Privacy

Simple Traffic Lights does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
